# Classroom Point Tracker Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 28, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 28, 2026
c
This Privacy Policy describes how the **Classroom Point Tracker** ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when used by authorized educators, administrators, and staff. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

---

## 1. Who We Are

The Classroom Point Tracker is an educational tool designed for use by authorized school staff to track, score, and report on student self-regulation and classroom behavior. The App is intended for institutional use only and is not directed to children under the age of 13 as end users.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the App and provide its educational functions.

### a. Account Information
When you register or log in, we collect:
- Full name
- Email address
- Password (stored as a securely hashed credential; we never see your plaintext password)
- Assigned role (e.g., teacher, administrator, staff)
- School and class assignments

### b. Student and Classroom Data
Authorized users enter and access classroom-related records, which may include:
- Student names and identifiers
- Class, school, and semester assignments
- Student level and behavior category designations
- Behavior codes and interval-based observations
- Daily and weekly performance summaries, scores, and color ratings
- Calendar events relevant to classroom schedules

This information is entered by authorized educators in the course of their professional duties and is treated as educational record data.

### c. Device and Usage Information
The App may collect limited technical information for proper functioning, including:
- Device type and operating system version
- Network connectivity status
- Local preferences and session state stored on your device

We do **not** collect advertising identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:
- Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions
- Provide role-based access to classes, students, and reports
- Record interval observations, calculate scores, and generate daily/weekly summaries
- Display rosters, reports, and trend data to authorized users
- Send in-app notifications and reminders related to scheduled observation intervals
- Maintain reliability, troubleshoot issues, and improve the App

We do **not** sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

## 4. How Information Is Stored and Protected

- Account and educational data are stored on secure cloud infrastructure provided by **Supabase**, our backend service provider.
- Communication between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS.
- Passwords and authentication tokens are managed using secure, industry-standard mechanisms (including device keychain storage where applicable).
- Access to student data is restricted by role-based permissions configured by school administrators.

While we take reasonable measures to protect information, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

## 5. Sharing of Information

Information collected through the App is accessed only by:
- The authorized educator, administrator, or staff member who entered or owns the record
- Other authorized staff at the same institution, based on assigned role permissions
- Our backend service provider (Supabase), solely for the purpose of hosting and operating the App

We may disclose information if required by law, valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users or the public.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is a tool for educators and is not offered directly to children. Any student data entered into the App is provided by authorized school personnel as part of their educational responsibilities and is governed by the policies of the educational institution that uses the App, including, where applicable, the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and similar laws.

## 7. Data Retention

Educational records are retained for as long as the institution requires the App for its educational purposes. Account information is retained while your account is active. Upon request from an authorized administrator or upon account deletion, associated data may be removed in accordance with the institution's policies and applicable law.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your role and your institution's policies, you may:
- Update your profile information within the App
- Request a password reset through the App
- Request that an administrator review, correct, or delete records associated with you
- Disable notifications through your device settings

If you have questions about specific records, please contact your school administrator first, as they manage data within the App on behalf of the institution.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party service:
- **Supabase** – authentication, database, and secure storage. Supabase's own privacy practices are available at https://supabase.com/privacy.

We do not integrate advertising networks or analytics SDKs that build user profiles for marketing.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, our practices, or legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
